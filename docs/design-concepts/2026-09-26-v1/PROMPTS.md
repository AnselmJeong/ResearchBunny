Persistent shell: small macOS traffic lights top left; tidy 205px light gray-green sidebar. Small simple forest-green bunny silhouette and exact wordmark "ResearchBunny". Project switcher "사회인지 연구". Sidebar icon and label navigation: "문헌 탐색", "내 아카이브", "검토 대기함", "핵심 문헌", "읽기 목록"; collection subsection "컬렉션" with "Interoception", "Social cognition"; below "탐색 이력". Content starts after sidebar, with compact 56px toolbar including back/forward arrows. Use Korean interface labels exactly as given, English article titles. Illustrative scholarly content, no unreadable gibberish. Distinguish screen selection from archive saving and seed setting. No cloud collaboration, AI chat, Zotero, citation drafting or other invented features.
